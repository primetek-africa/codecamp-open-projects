# Alpha Classroom 2027 Capstone Project Selection

This is the curated list of capstone projects for the Alpha classroom of Primetek Academy Codecamp. Every project targets a concrete African pain point, has a realistic paying customer, and can be delivered as a working MVP in four to eight weeks by a student or small team.

All projects are web and mobile friendly. That means a responsive web app, a progressive web app, or a React Native or Flutter mobile app paired with a web dashboard. No desktop or embedded hardware projects are listed. Students should favor low bandwidth friendly design, offline tolerance where it matters, SMS or WhatsApp channels where they are the right fit, and mobile money as the default payment method.

The suggested technology stack is a hint, not a rule. Our program is built around JavaScript, Vue, React, Next, Nuxt, Node, PHP, Laravel, Python, Django, GraphQL, MySQL, PostgreSQL and MongoDB. Teams may swap in tools their team is comfortable with, provided the architecture notes are updated accordingly.

Assignments are currently open. Students should validate the target user and willingness to pay with at least five real interviews before building, pick one pilot location and one primary user journey, and cut scope before cutting testing. Health, fintech, crypto and safety projects must stay inside sandbox or simulation mode for the classroom MVP and must not hold customer funds or present an unlicensed service as a bank, clinic, insurer or payment provider.

## Delivery guidance

Use the listed estimate as a planning range and cut scope before cutting testing. Interview at least five intended users, agree on one pilot location and one primary user journey, then build only what is needed to test that journey. A four to six week MVP should favor a responsive web app or PWA with a small web dashboard, manual review where needed, and a small set of clear workflows. Mobile native features, automated integrations, AI features beyond a single call, and additional regions should be added only when the pilot proves they are useful.

Use sandbox or mock payment flows and sandbox crypto chains for a classroom MVP. Do not hold customer funds or present an unlicensed service as a bank, insurer, clinic or payment provider. Health projects should protect personal information, use qualified professionals for clinical content, and avoid diagnosing users. Safety, civic and surveillance projects should minimize personal data, avoid public exposure of sensitive locations, keep an audit log for moderators, and never record audio or video without clear user consent.

## Shared capabilities across projects

Many of these projects share the same building blocks. Teams are encouraged to borrow and reuse approaches. Common blocks include authentication with phone OTP, mobile money payment via Paystack, Flutterwave or M-Pesa Daraja, SMS and WhatsApp via Africa's Talking or Twilio, map tiles via Mapbox or OpenStreetMap, media storage via Cloudinary or an S3 compatible bucket, background jobs via BullMQ, Laravel Queues or Celery, and AI features via Claude, GPT or Hugging Face hosted models with response caching to keep cost predictable.
