# CatchConnect Fish Market

## The idea
Connect small-scale fishers with nearby restaurants and traders using timely catch listings and planned pickups. Start with one landing site and a limited number of species.

## Users and value
Fishers find buyers before landing; businesses can plan purchases. Revenue can come from seller subscriptions or a transparent fee on confirmed orders.

## MVP scope
- Fisher and buyer profiles with preferred landing area.
- Catch listing with species, estimated quantity, landing time, and price.
- Buyer order request and fisher confirmation.
- Pickup status and order history.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. Design for mobile use and patchy connectivity. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not guarantee catch volume, freshness, or transport. Use clear estimated quantity labels and test how users handle variable catch sizes.
