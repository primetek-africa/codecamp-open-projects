# MarketDay Produce Link

## The idea
Help small farmers advertise harvests to nearby shops, restaurants, and households before market day. Start in one market area and focus on a few common crops so buyers can compare useful listings without depending on a large marketplace.

## Users and value
Farmers get more buyer enquiries and can plan harvest quantities. Local buyers spend less time sourcing produce. Revenue can come from a modest seller subscription or a fee on confirmed orders.

## MVP scope
- Farmer and buyer profiles with a selected market area.
- Listings with crop, quantity, unit, price, harvest date, and availability.
- Buyer requests and farmer order confirmation with pickup details.
- Listing status and basic order history.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. Build a responsive web app or progressive web app. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL or MongoDB. Messaging and payment integrations can be added after a pilot; begin with in-app status updates and recorded payment method.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not promise guaranteed prices, logistics, or payment escrow. Verify units and pickup expectations with local users.
