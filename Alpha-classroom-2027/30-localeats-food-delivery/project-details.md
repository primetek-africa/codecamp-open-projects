# LocalEats Food Delivery

A hyperlocal food ordering and delivery platform for neighborhood restaurants, chop bars, mama put stalls and home cooks.

## Problem

Large food delivery platforms take 25 to 35 percent commission and prioritize larger restaurants. A home cook or a chop bar cannot survive on those margins and cannot afford a dedicated courier. Customers in student hostels and residential estates want their familiar local food on demand but have no good app for it.

LocalEats is a low commission neighborhood first delivery app. Delivery runs on JiraniDelivery or on the restaurant's own rider.

## Target users

Primary users are hostel students, residents of estates and office workers. Secondary users are restaurants, chop bars, mama put, home cooks. Tertiary users are couriers.

## MVP features

1. Restaurant onboarding. Business, menu, photos.
2. Menu management. Items, prices, availability.
3. Customer order. Cart, checkout with mobile money.
4. Delivery handoff. Order goes to the restaurant kitchen and a courier is auto assigned.
5. Order tracking. Status updates.
6. Review and tip.
7. Weekly settlement with a low commission.

## Post MVP ideas

Group orders for hostels, meal plans, allergy filters, integration with event pop ups, dark kitchen support.

## User stories

As a student I want jollof and goat meat from my corner stall on a Saturday. As a home cook I want to accept orders without building my own app. As a courier I want enough orders in my neighborhood to make a shift worthwhile.

## Success metrics

Active restaurants, orders per day, average delivery time, repeat customers, restaurant retention.

## Revenue model

Low commission per order (5 to 10 percent), restaurant subscription for featured placement, sponsored cards.

## Risks and compliance

Food safety is a public health concern. Verify restaurants. Allow customers to flag issues. Keep allergen disclosure available. Do not advertise raw or undercooked items without a warning.
