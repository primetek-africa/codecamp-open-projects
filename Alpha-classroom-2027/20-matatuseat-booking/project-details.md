# MatatuSeat Booking

## The idea
Help participating shuttle or intercity operators publish departures and confirm seat requests. The MVP supports selected operators and routes, not every informal stop or route.

## Users and value
Passengers can see departure details and request a seat; operators can organize bookings. Revenue may come from an operator subscription or a transparent booking fee.

## MVP scope
- Operator accounts and route/departure listings.
- Seat availability entered and managed by the operator.
- Passenger booking request and operator confirmation.
- Booking reference, cancellation status, and fee record.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **6 to 8 weeks**. Use a phone-first responsive app. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL. Keep payment as pay-at-operator or a recorded status for the pilot.

## Keep out of the first release
Confirm operator and route information locally. Do not guarantee departure times or seat availability until the operator confirms the booking.
