# PharmaFind Stock Locator

A map and search app that lets patients find nearby pharmacies with specific medicines in stock.

## Problem

In many African cities, finding paracetamol, insulin, antibiotics or specific chronic medication can take a full day of hopping from pharmacy to pharmacy. Prices vary widely between outlets. For a parent with a sick child or a diabetic patient, this is a serious problem.

PharmaFind lets a patient search for a medicine, see nearby pharmacies that have it, compare price and reserve a pack before heading out.

## Target users

Primary users are patients looking for a specific medicine, especially caregivers of chronic patients. Secondary users are independent pharmacies that want more foot traffic.

## MVP features

1. Pharmacy onboarding. Phone verification, pharmacy license number, address and map pin.
2. Product search. By brand name, generic name or active ingredient.
3. Stock status. Pharmacy staff update stock in bulk from a CSV or one by one. Stock has a visible timestamp.
4. Reservation. Patient can request a reservation. Pharmacy confirms within a time window.
5. In app chat. Quick message between patient and pharmacy.
6. Moderation. Admin removes bogus listings and verifies licenses.

## Post MVP ideas

Prescription upload, home delivery via JiraniDelivery, pharmacy benefits manager integration, chronic refill subscription, price index insights sold to media or research.

## User stories

As a caregiver I want to find insulin within 3 kilometers of me so my relative does not miss a dose. As a pharmacist I want more customers to walk in when I have stock that others do not.

## Success metrics

Searches per day, reserved and completed pickups, pharmacies active in the last 7 days, patient repeat use.

## Revenue model

Paid pharmacy listing, paid priority ranking in a result list, promoted medicines sponsored by a licensed distributor with disclosure.

## Risks and compliance

Verify pharmacy licenses. Do not accept controlled substances for reservation. Do not display dosage advice. Always recommend talking to a pharmacist. Keep prescription images encrypted and delete after a short retention window.
