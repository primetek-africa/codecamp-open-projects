# WaterWise Irrigation Scheduler

## The idea
Help a small farming group coordinate shared water slots, pumps, or irrigation equipment. The MVP improves scheduling and record keeping rather than measuring water automatically.

## Users and value
Farmers and water-point managers can reduce schedule conflicts and make usage visible. Revenue could be a cooperative subscription or a small equipment-provider service fee.

## MVP scope
- Farm plot and shared resource records.
- Slot requests and a manager-approved weekly schedule.
- Usage entries, reminders, and missed-slot reporting.
- Basic subscription status for participating groups.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Build as a responsive web app; allow managers to print a weekly schedule. Optional stack: Vue or React, PHP or Node.js, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not calculate agronomic irrigation needs or promise water availability. Agree local allocation rules with users and show schedule changes transparently.
