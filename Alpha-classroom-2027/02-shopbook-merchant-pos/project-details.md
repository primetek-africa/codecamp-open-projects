# ShopBook Merchant POS

A simple point of sale, inventory and customer credit book for informal retailers who run their business on a smartphone.

## Problem

An urban kiosk, duka, provision store or hair salon in Lagos, Nairobi, Accra or Dakar typically operates on memory and a paper notebook. Owners cannot answer three basic questions. What were my sales today. Which products are running out. Who owes me money and how much. Without this, they cannot plan restocking, cannot grow and cannot access credit because they have no verifiable record.

ShopBook gives the owner a one screen workflow to record a sale in under 10 seconds, track customer credit and see a daily summary.

## Target users

Primary users are owner operators of small retail outlets with revenue between the equivalent of 100 and 2000 US dollars per day. Secondary users are part time staff and informal lenders who want to see merchant performance before giving credit.

## MVP features

1. Product catalogue. Add product with name, buying price, selling price and current stock.
2. Sales entry. One tap to add a product to a current sale, support cash or mobile money reference, print or send receipt by SMS.
3. Customer credit book. Record a sale on credit against a customer name and phone, mark partial or full payment later.
4. Daily cash summary. Opening cash, total sales, cash in, mobile money in, cash out, closing cash.
5. Low stock alert. Flag products below a threshold.
6. Simple reports. Best selling products, top customers, sales by day.
7. Multi staff toggle on paid tier. Owner can invite a cashier with a limited role.

## Post MVP ideas

Automatic mobile money reconciliation with M-Pesa or Paystack daily statements, barcode scanning via the phone camera, expense tracking, loan application integration with KopaCredit, WhatsApp receipt share, offline first mode with sync.

## User stories

As a kiosk owner I want to see exactly how much my shop made yesterday so I can plan my restock. As a cashier I want to add a sale in under 10 seconds so I never slow a customer down. As a customer I want a receipt on my phone so I can prove I paid.

## Success metrics

Daily active merchants, sales recorded per merchant per day, customer credit records closed, retention after 60 days, upgrade rate to paid plan.

## Revenue model

Freemium. Free tier covers a single user with sales and credit tracking. Paid tier adds multi staff, reports and SMS receipts.

## Risks and compliance

Do not store card data. Record mobile money transactions by reference only. Clearly mark that ShopBook is not an accounting system and not tax advice. In Kenya and Nigeria, keep an eye on ETIMS and FIRS requirements for small business invoicing and offer an export friendly format.
