# ClassLink Parent Updates

## The idea
Give small schools a practical channel for attendance notices and important class updates to caregivers. Begin with announcements and manual delivery records, then test SMS or messaging integrations.

## Users and value
Schools reduce repeated calls and caregivers receive relevant updates. A per-school subscription can cover administrator tools and message allowances.

## MVP scope
- School, class, learner, and caregiver contact records.
- Staff-entered attendance notices.
- Class and school announcements with delivery status.
- Caregiver contact preferences and opt-out.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. Build a responsive staff dashboard with a simple caregiver view. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Protect minors' data, restrict messages to authorized caregivers, and do not send sensitive details in notifications. Confirm consent and school communication policies.
