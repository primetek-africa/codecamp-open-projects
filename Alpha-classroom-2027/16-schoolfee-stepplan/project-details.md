# SchoolFee StepPlan

## The idea
Help schools and families see a clear schedule for paying school fees in agreed installments. The MVP records payments confirmed by school staff and does not process or hold funds.

## Users and value
Families can understand upcoming amounts; school administrators can track balances and follow-ups. Revenue may come from a school subscription.

## MVP scope
- School and student records with caregiver contacts.
- Staff-created installment schedule and due dates.
- Payment receipt entry and current balance view.
- Parent reminders and downloadable statement.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Use a responsive web app that staff can access from phones or a computer. Optional stack: React or Vue, PHP or Node.js, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not collect fees directly or store full payment credentials. Validate balances with school staff and limit student data visibility to authorized caregivers.
