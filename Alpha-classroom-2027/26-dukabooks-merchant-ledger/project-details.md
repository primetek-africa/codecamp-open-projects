# DukaBooks Merchant Ledger

## The idea
Give small retailers a clear daily record of sales, expenses, and customer credit. The MVP is a private bookkeeping tool that works well on a phone.

## Users and value
Shop owners can review cash flow and outstanding balances without relying on memory or loose paper. Revenue can come from a low-cost monthly plan or paid export features.

## MVP scope
- Private shop account and daily sales and expense entries.
- Customer credit balances and repayment entries.
- Simple receipt or transaction view.
- Daily and monthly cash-flow summary.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. Use a responsive progressive web app and simple, low-data forms. Optional stack: Vue or React, PHP or Node.js, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not store bank credentials or make lending decisions. Design for corrections and backups, and make clear that the ledger is not formal tax or accounting advice.
