# WardFix Civic Reporter

A civic issue reporting and tracking platform that connects residents to local government.

## Problem

Residents see a pothole, a broken street light, a water leak or illegal dumping and have no simple way to report it. If they call the ward office, the complaint disappears. City councils receive the same issue from dozens of residents with no way to deduplicate or track.

WardFix lets a resident file a report with a photo and location, see the status timeline and follow their ward's dashboard. It gives the ward office a clean inbox and a public accountability trail.

## Target users

Primary users are residents of urban wards. Secondary users are ward councillors and city service departments. Tertiary users are civic tech NGOs and media houses.

## MVP features

1. Issue report with category, photo and location.
2. Category and ward auto detection.
3. Status timeline. Reported, acknowledged, in progress, resolved.
4. Public ward dashboard. Count and type of issues by month.
5. Agency response. Ward officer can post an update.
6. Email alerts to the agency inbox.
7. Elected representative view. Councillors see their ward trend.

## Post MVP ideas

Public consultations, participatory budgeting, broken service clusters surfaced by AI, SMS report via a short code, open data export.

## User stories

As a resident I want to report a pothole in 30 seconds. As a ward officer I want a daily digest of new issues by category. As a councillor I want a weekly report for my ward.

## Success metrics

Reports filed, issues acknowledged, issues resolved, median time to resolution, repeat reporters.

## Revenue model

Municipal license, civic foundation grants, data access license for research.

## Risks and compliance

Protect reporter identity. Moderation to prevent political abuse. Do not surface individual street addresses on public views. Partnership with recognized civic groups adds legitimacy.
