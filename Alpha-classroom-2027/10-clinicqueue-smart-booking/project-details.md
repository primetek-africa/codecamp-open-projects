# ClinicQueue Smart Booking

An appointment and digital queue management system for outpatient clinics.

## Problem

In public and private clinics across Africa, patients arrive at dawn and sit for hours because there is no appointment system. Clinic staff juggle paper registers and verbal numbers. Patients cannot leave to run errands and come back, because they will lose their place.

ClinicQueue lets clinics offer booked slots, hand out digital queue tokens and show a live status board that patients can watch from their phone.

## Target users

Primary users are small to mid size outpatient clinics, dental and optical practices. Secondary users are patients. Tertiary users are clinic chains that run multiple branches.

## MVP features

1. Clinic profile. Branch, services, opening hours.
2. Appointment request. Patient picks service and date. Clinic confirms.
3. Digital queue. Walk ins can be given a token on arrival. The token is a QR and a short code.
4. Live queue board. Shows the current number served and the waiting count. Patients can watch from their phone.
5. Visit status. Patient status flows from waiting to in consultation to done.
6. Admin view. Staff manage slots, reassign, mark no shows.
7. No show tracking. Flag patients who miss repeated appointments.

## Post MVP ideas

SMS alerts when your position is close, ClinicQueue Insights dashboard, triage based priority routing, integration with ClinicAnalytics for average service time, patient feedback after the visit.

## User stories

As a patient I want to see that I am number 8 in the queue so that I can run a quick errand. As a receptionist I want to assign a walk in token in 5 seconds. As a clinic manager I want to see the average waiting time by day.

## Success metrics

Clinics onboarded, daily tokens issued, average waiting time measured and reduced, no show rate, patient repeat use.

## Revenue model

Per branch monthly subscription. Premium plan for analytics and multi branch. Hardware kiosk upsell is possible later but not in the MVP.

## Risks and compliance

Do not store clinical data in the MVP. Keep patient profile minimal, with name, phone and visit reason category only. Comply with each country's data protection act. Make clear the system does not replace triage.
