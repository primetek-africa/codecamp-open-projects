# MamaFollow Care Reminders

## The idea
Help clinics coordinate routine maternal care reminders and follow-up with people who opt in. The service supports a clinic workflow and does not replace professional care.

## Users and value
Clinic staff can identify missed visits and follow up; patients receive reminders through a preferred channel. A clinic or health organization can pay for a subscription.

## MVP scope
- Clinic-managed patient contact record with explicit opt-in.
- Staff-entered visit schedule and reminder preferences.
- Reminder queue with delivery status, using mock or manual delivery initially.
- Clinic follow-up list and referral contact information.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. A responsive web app can be tested before SMS integration. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or Python, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not provide diagnosis or medical recommendations. Use clinic-approved reminder content, restrict access, and design for consent, opt-out, and confidential contact preferences.
