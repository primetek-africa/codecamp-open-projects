# ReuseRoute Waste Exchange

## The idea
Help businesses and households find local recyclers or reuse organizations for materials they can collect. Focus on a few material types and a small pilot area.

## Users and value
Waste generators find alternatives to disposal; collectors receive leads and can plan pickups. Revenue could be a listing subscription, sponsored recycler profile, or fee per completed collection.

## MVP scope
- Generator and recycler profiles with accepted material types.
- Material listing with approximate quantity and collection area.
- Pickup request, assignment, and status updates.
- Basic records of completed collections and service fees.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Build a responsive web app, with list-based location search before adding maps. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not claim materials are recycled unless the receiving partner confirms it. Verify local handling requirements and avoid unsafe waste categories in the pilot.
