# FloodAlert Community

A community reporting and warning app for seasonal floods, blocked roads and overflowing drains.

## Problem

Urban and periurban residents in Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Dar es Salaam and Dakar face recurring flooding with growing climate pressure. Residents are caught off guard, lose property and sometimes lives. Municipal responders act on fragmented reports. Early warning exists only for large events.

FloodAlert gives residents a way to report local flooding and blocked routes, see a map of active alerts and subscribe to area alerts by SMS.

## Target users

Primary users are urban residents in flood prone neighborhoods. Secondary users are municipal disaster offices, Red Cross chapters and NGOs. Tertiary users are commuters planning routes.

## MVP features

1. Report an incident. Flood depth category, blocked road, water pooling.
2. Photo and short note.
3. Map of active alerts per city.
4. Verification by moderators.
5. SMS subscription per ward or neighborhood.
6. Responder dashboard. List of unresolved reports.
7. Trend view by season.

## Post MVP ideas

Weather data overlay from a public feed, safe route suggestion, school and health facility closure notices, integration with the city traffic feed, SOS escalation.

## User stories

As a resident I want to know if my street is flooded before I leave home. As a responder I want a list of today's unresolved reports. As an NGO I want trend data for my annual report.

## Success metrics

Active reporters, verified incidents, SMS subscribers, resolved reports, response time.

## Revenue model

Grant funding, municipal license, paid responder dashboard for NGOs.

## Risks and compliance

Do not misrepresent severity. Keep user data minimal. Do not publish fine grained home locations. Allow users to report anonymously.

## Research references
