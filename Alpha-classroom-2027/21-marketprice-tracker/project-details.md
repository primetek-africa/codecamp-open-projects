# MarketPrice Tracker

A daily market price tracker for staple foods across African markets.

## Problem

Farmers, traders and households do not know the price of maize, tomato, onion, cassava or fish in neighboring markets. The result is that farmers sell at a loss and buyers overpay. Public data from ministries lags by weeks. Mobile prices through SMS exist but are patchy.

MarketPrice Tracker collects daily price inputs from a network of field agents across markets and makes them available by web, SMS and a weekly report.

## Target users

Primary users are smallholder farmers, retail traders and cooperatives. Secondary users are media houses and researchers. Tertiary users are food security agencies.

## MVP features

1. Market directory. Named markets with location.
2. Daily price entry. Field agents enter prices for selected products.
3. Admin verification. Outliers flagged for review.
4. Trend charts. Line charts of prices over the last 30 and 90 days.
5. SMS price alert. A user subscribes to a product and market, gets a short SMS per day or when the price changes by more than a threshold.
6. Weekly market report. Auto generated PDF per region.

## Post MVP ideas

USSD menu for low end phones, buyer and seller directory, forward price forecast with ML, data licensing portal, integration with FarmLink.

## User stories

As a farmer I want to know the maize price in three nearby markets before deciding where to sell. As a trader I want a short SMS each morning with the prices I care about. As a food security officer I want a weekly PDF for my region.

## Success metrics

Markets covered, daily entries, SMS subscribers, media partnership deals, data license deals.

## Revenue model

SMS alert subscription, cooperative license, media and research data license, sponsored report pages.

## Risks and compliance

Price data can move markets. Only publish after moderation. Attribute each entry to a verified source. Do not accept prices from anonymous users without a moderation path.
