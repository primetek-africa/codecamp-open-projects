# Community Water Point Monitor

## The idea
Give residents, water vendors, and local maintenance teams a shared view of water point status and reported repairs. Start with a directory and manually confirmed updates.

## Users and value
Residents can check service status before traveling; vendors and maintenance teams can share availability and coordinate repairs. Revenue may come from a vendor service listing or organization subscription.

## MVP scope
- Water point directory with public status and last-updated time.
- User outage report and administrator verification queue.
- Repair records and responsible contact.
- Vendor contact and service dashboard for a selected area.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Design a low-data mobile web interface with an area list and optional map. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not represent unverified reports as official notices. Confirm who can publish a verified status and how outdated information is marked.
