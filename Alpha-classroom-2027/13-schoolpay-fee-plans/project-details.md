# SchoolPay Fee Plans

A school fee installment plan platform for private schools and informal academies.

## Problem

Across Africa, private schools and informal academies see up to one third of fees paid late or not at all. Families cannot pay a full term in one go but schools lack a transparent installment system that holds everyone to a plan. Families also cannot easily see their current balance.

SchoolPay lets a school build a fee plan per student, collect by mobile money or cash with a receipt, and give parents a clear balance view with reminders.

## Target users

Primary users are private schools, informal academies and vocational colleges with between 50 and 2000 learners. Secondary users are parents and guardians.

## MVP features

1. School onboarding. Admin signs up, adds classes and students.
2. Student record. Minimal profile with parent contact.
3. Fee plan. Per term or per year with installment schedule.
4. Payment capture. Staff records a cash or mobile money payment with a reference. Mobile money capture is reference only in the MVP.
5. Parent balance view. A phone friendly page the parent opens from an SMS link.
6. Reminders. SMS or WhatsApp before each due date.
7. Arrears report. School sees who is behind and by how much.

## Post MVP ideas

Direct mobile money collection via Paystack or M-Pesa, parent app with payment history and auto debit, school scholarship ledger, learner attendance link, government stipend reconciliation.

## User stories

As a bursar I want to record a mobile money payment in under 15 seconds. As a parent I want to see how much I still owe without calling the school. As a head teacher I want to know which families are two installments behind.

## Success metrics

Active schools, students on an active plan, on time installment rate, SMS open rate, arrears reduction over a term.

## Revenue model

Per student fee or per installment collected. Setup fee for migration. Premium plan with analytics and parent app.

## Risks and compliance

Do not hold funds in the MVP. Record payments against a school owned mobile money till or bank account. Keep learner data minimal. Respect data protection in each country.

## Research references

National private schools associations, World Bank studies on school fee affordability, case studies on Zeraki, Shule Direct and Flux school payments.
