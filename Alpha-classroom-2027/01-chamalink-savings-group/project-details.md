# ChamaLink Savings Group

A shared ledger web and mobile app for rotating savings groups known as chama in Kenya, susu in Ghana, tontine in francophone Africa, esusu in Nigeria and VSLA in Uganda and Rwanda.

## Problem

Rotating savings and credit associations power grassroots finance across Africa. Most still run on paper exercise books managed by one treasurer. That creates four recurring problems. Members argue about who paid what and when. Loans and repayments are forgotten or double counted. Treasurers lose time preparing meeting reports. Groups with a dishonest treasurer can lose months of savings before anyone notices.

ChamaLink gives every member a read view of the same ledger and gives the treasurer tools to record contributions, loans and meeting decisions without using spreadsheets.

## Target users

Primary users are informal savings groups of 5 to 60 members in urban and peri-urban communities. Secondary users are NGOs and SACCOs that support hundreds of groups and want oversight and a program level report.

## MVP features

1. Group creation and invite. The treasurer creates a group, picks contribution frequency, and invites members by phone number.
2. Member register. Each member has a profile with phone number, role and join date.
3. Contribution ledger. Treasurer records each contribution with amount, date, meeting reference and mobile money transaction code if any.
4. Loan request and approval. A member requests a loan with purpose and term. The group approves inside the app. Repayments post against the loan with interest if configured.
5. Meeting minutes. Short structured notes per meeting including attendance, agenda items and decisions.
6. Exportable statement. A group or member can download a PDF statement for a selected period.
7. SMS notification. Members receive a reminder before each meeting and a confirmation after each contribution.

## Post MVP ideas

Direct mobile money collection via M-Pesa or Flutterwave, in app payouts, auto reconciliation of mobile money statements, multi group view for SACCOs, audit trail download, offline capture for rural groups, dividend calculation.

## User stories

As a treasurer I want to record a contribution in under 15 seconds so that I can finish the register during the meeting. As a member I want to see my balance at any time so that I never need to ask the treasurer. As an NGO program officer I want to see which of my 80 groups missed a meeting last month so that I know who to visit.

## Success metrics

Active groups holding at least one meeting per month, average time to record a contribution, number of exported statements, retention of groups after three months, net promoter score from treasurers.

## Revenue model

Flat monthly fee per active group in local currency. Premium plan for groups above 30 members with multi device access and NGO oversight. White label license for SACCOs and NGOs.

## Risks and compliance

Do not collect or hold funds in the MVP. All money moves outside the app via existing mobile money. Keep member data minimal. Treat the exported statement as a record, not a legal certificate. Avoid interest rate advertising that could be classified as a lending product.
