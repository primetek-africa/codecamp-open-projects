# BloodMatch Donor Registry

A geo aware blood donor matching platform for hospitals and emergency requests.

## Problem

Blood shortages are a daily problem in most African hospitals. When a patient needs a rare type, the hospital or family calls friends, posts on WhatsApp and prays. A verified pool of willing local donors with the right blood type does not exist in most cities. As a result, patients wait, and preventable deaths happen.

BloodMatch builds a trusted donor register per city and lets hospitals send a geo scoped notification when a matching donor is needed.

## Target users

Primary users are voluntary donors in urban areas. Secondary users are blood banks and hospitals. Tertiary users are NGOs and campaigns like Red Cross drives.

## MVP features

1. Donor sign up. Phone, blood type, city, age and basic eligibility questions.
2. Donor consent and cooldown. Donors confirm they are willing to be called. After each donation the record enters a 90 day cooldown.
3. Hospital account. Hospital verifies with a staff email and uploads a verification letter.
4. Request creation. Hospital selects blood type, urgency, city and quantity.
5. Geo scoped notification. Push, SMS or WhatsApp notification to eligible donors within the chosen radius.
6. Donor response. Available, not available or snooze.
7. Donation record. Hospital confirms the donation, sets the cooldown.
8. Public awareness feed. Anonymized counter of requests served and lives potentially saved.

## Post MVP ideas

Integration with national blood service databases, mobile drive scheduling, employer donor campaigns, gamified donor streaks, pre screening questions for faster intake.

## User stories

As a donor I want to be called only when my blood type and my city match a real need. As a hospital intake nurse I want to send a request to 200 eligible donors nearby in under two minutes. As a public health officer I want to see request response rates over time.

## Success metrics

Verified donors, verified hospitals, response rate per request, successful donations confirmed, repeat donations.

## Revenue model

NGO and blood service partnership fees, sponsored awareness drives, employer campaign plans.

## Risks and compliance

Blood donation is medically regulated. Partner with the national blood transfusion service in each country. Never present the platform as a clinical diagnostic tool. Protect donor data. Never share a donor's phone number without consent. Allow donors to opt out instantly and permanently.
