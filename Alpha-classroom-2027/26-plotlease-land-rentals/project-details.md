# PlotLease Land Rentals

A short term land and plot rental marketplace for urban farmers, event organizers and small developers.

## Problem

Across periurban Africa, many landowners have idle plots. At the same time, urban farmers, event organizers, builders and informal traders need land for a season, a weekend or a quarter. There is no transparent way to find each other. Deals are done by word of mouth and often collapse because the terms are not written down.

PlotLease lists plots with clear terms and books them for short term use.

## Target users

Primary users are landowners with unused plots. Secondary users are urban farmers, event organizers and small developers. Tertiary users are letting agents.

## MVP features

1. Plot listing. Photos, location, size, available dates, asking price per day or per month.
2. Allowed uses. Farming, event, parking, storage.
3. Enquiry flow. Interested renter submits a request.
4. Lease term checklist. Agreed start, end, deposit, insurance disclosure.
5. Booking with a small platform fee.
6. Rating after lease ends.

## Post MVP ideas

Legal template library, land title verification with an open registry, insurance bolt on, marketplace of service providers like tent rental and security.

## User stories

As a landowner I want to earn from an idle plot without selling. As a renter I want a clear lease without paying a broker. As a letting agent I want a wider pool of plots to offer my clients.

## Success metrics

Active listings, enquiries per listing, confirmed bookings, average lease value, repeat landowners.

## Revenue model

Service fee per lease, premium listing, service provider referrals.

## Risks and compliance

Do not represent as a land title verifier. Encourage physical visits before lease signing. Keep landowner ID images encrypted. Comply with country land use regulation.
