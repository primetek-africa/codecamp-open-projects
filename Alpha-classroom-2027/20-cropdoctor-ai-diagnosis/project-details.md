# CropDoctor AI Diagnosis

An AI powered crop pest and disease diagnosis app for smallholder farmers and extension officers.

## Problem

Across Africa, farmers lose up to 40 percent of a harvest to pests and crop diseases. Many of these are preventable if caught early, but there are not enough extension officers to visit every farm. Online pest guides are too generic, in the wrong language and cost data to open on a cheap phone.

CropDoctor lets a farmer take a photo of a symptom, get a likely diagnosis from an AI model trained on local crops and receive treatment advice reviewed by an agronomist.

## Target users

Primary users are smallholder farmers growing maize, cassava, tomato, cowpea, rice and banana. Secondary users are extension officers. Tertiary users are agribusinesses that want branded tools for their farmer networks.

## MVP features

1. Camera first capture. Easy photo flow for a single leaf or fruit.
2. AI diagnosis. Model returns a ranked list of likely pests or diseases with a confidence score.
3. Treatment card. Short, approved advice for each diagnosis, available in English, French and one local language.
4. Follow up. A reminder to check back in 7 days.
5. Expert referral. If confidence is low, the farmer can request a chat with an extension officer.
6. Offline capture. Store photos without connectivity and sync when online.
7. History. Farmer sees past diagnoses per plot.

## Post MVP ideas

Field level outbreak map for extension networks, input marketplace connection, voice diagnosis in local languages, integration with ClimateFarm AI and FarmLink, white label for agribusiness.

## User stories

As a farmer I want to know if the spots on my maize leaves are rust or something worse. As an extension officer I want to see which villages are reporting symptoms this week. As an agribusiness I want to brand a version of this app for 20000 farmers.

## Success metrics

Diagnoses per week, accuracy against expert review, treatment follow up rate, expert chat usage, retention after 2 planting cycles.

## Revenue model

NGO licensing, agribusiness branded version, premium farmer subscription with priority expert chat.

## Risks and compliance

AI diagnosis can be wrong. Show the confidence score and a note that the farmer should consult an extension officer for serious cases. Keep treatment advice reviewed by a qualified agronomist. Avoid recommending banned pesticides.
