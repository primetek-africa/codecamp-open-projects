# FarmLink Produce Market

A direct from farm marketplace connecting smallholder farmers with urban retailers, hotels, caterers and consumer buying groups.

## Problem

Smallholder farmers sell cheap at the farm gate because the next buyer is a middleman. Urban buyers pay high prices because the supply chain adds several hands. Both sides want to meet in the middle but do not know how.

FarmLink gives farmers a listing with photos and quantities, lets buyers request stock and handles pickup or delivery through an agreed partner.

## Target users

Primary users are smallholder farmers producing maize, cassava, tomato, onion, potato, banana, mango, avocado, dairy and poultry. Secondary users are urban retailers, hotels, caterers and consumer buying groups. Tertiary users are cooperative union leaders.

## MVP features

1. Farmer listing. Crop, grade, quantity, farm location, photos, available from date, asking price.
2. Buyer request. Crop, quantity, budget, delivery or pickup, needed by date.
3. Negotiation. In app message between farmer and buyer with offer and counter.
4. Order confirmation. Agreed quantity, price, pickup window.
5. Delivery option. Hand off to a partner logistics provider.
6. Rating. After a confirmed order.
7. Dispute flag. Escalate to a human moderator.

## Post MVP ideas

Cold chain tracking with FreshRoute, aggregation by cooperative, forward contracts, input credit tied to a confirmed buyer, quality grading with CropDoctor AI.

## User stories

As a farmer I want a buyer to offer a better price than the middleman. As a restaurant chef I want 40 kilograms of tomato next week with a clear price. As a cooperative I want to coordinate orders across 20 members.

## Success metrics

Active farmers, buyer requests, confirmed orders, order value, dispute rate.

## Revenue model

Commission per confirmed order. Farmer premium listing. Buyer subscription for priority alerts.

## Risks and compliance

Validate the farmer by location and reference. Discourage listings that make quality claims the farm cannot back. For perishables, ensure the pickup window matches supply reality to avoid loss.
