# Community Health Referral Tracker

## The idea
Give community health workers a minimal way to refer a client to a nearby facility and confirm that follow-up took place. Pilot with one organization and a small set of facilities.

## Users and value
Health workers can track open referrals; facilities can share status with authorized referrers. A health organization may pay for a managed deployment or subscription.

## MVP scope
- Facility directory and contact details.
- Minimal referral record with consent and non-sensitive category.
- Referral status and follow-up reminder.
- Aggregate activity report without names.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Build a mobile-friendly web app with a low-bandwidth workflow. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or Python, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not store clinical case notes or expose referrals publicly. Agree on consent, access roles, secure devices, and data retention with the participating organization.
