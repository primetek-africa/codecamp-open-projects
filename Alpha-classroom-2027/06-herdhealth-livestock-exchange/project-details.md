# HerdHealth & Livestock Exchange

## The idea
Combine a simple livestock care record with local sale listings and a directory of animal-health service providers. Begin with one livestock type and one service area.

## Users and value
Keepers organize animal records and reach buyers or providers; buyers and service providers find relevant local listings. Revenue may come from listing upgrades or provider subscriptions.

## MVP scope
- Animal profiles with species, approximate age, and owner notes.
- Care and vaccination reminders entered by the keeper or provider.
- Sale listings and buyer enquiry flow.
- Directory of service providers with contact and verification status.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. A mobile-first progressive web app supports field use. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL or MongoDB.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not offer veterinary diagnosis or certify animal health. Make provider verification clear and have qualified practitioners supply health guidance.
