# EventPass Mobile Ticketing

A mobile ticketing platform for concerts, campus events, church services, sports and local festivals.

## Problem

Event organizers across Africa still rely on paper tickets, Excel sheets and WhatsApp bank screenshots. Tickets get copied. Revenue leaks. Attendance counts are wrong. Buyers waste time standing in a cash line at the gate.

EventPass lets organizers sell tickets by mobile money with a QR code and scan them at the gate.

## Target users

Primary users are small event promoters, student unions, churches, sports clubs and cultural organizations. Secondary users are attendees. Tertiary users are venues that host events.

## MVP features

1. Event listing. Name, date, venue, poster image.
2. Ticket tiers. Early bird, regular, VIP.
3. Checkout with mobile money and card.
4. QR ticket delivered via WhatsApp or SMS.
5. Usher scanner app on a phone. Scans QR, prevents double scan.
6. Sales dashboard. Tickets sold, revenue, refunds.
7. Refund flag for admin review.

## Post MVP ideas

Door hardware for high volume events, dynamic pricing, seat maps, promoter payouts, resale with anti scalping cap, affiliate links.

## User stories

As a promoter I want to sell 500 tickets on WhatsApp and have a scanner at the gate. As an attendee I want a QR on my phone that works even offline. As a church bursar I want a weekly sales report.

## Success metrics

Events run, tickets sold, scan success rate, refund rate, repeat promoters.

## Revenue model

Service fee per paid ticket, premium promoter plan with analytics, white label for venues.

## Risks and compliance

Mobile money reconciliation must be perfect. Keep ticket secrets non guessable. Prevent one QR from being scanned twice. Do not store card data.
