# BodaGo Ride Hailing

A mobile first ride hailing platform for motorbike taxis known as boda boda in East Africa, okada in Nigeria and bendskin in Cameroon.

## Problem

Motorbike taxis move millions of commuters every day. Most trips are negotiated at the kerb, pricing is opaque, there is no safety record, and riders have no way to grow a repeat customer base. A few large apps exist but they focus on cities where mobile money and formal regulation are well developed. Many corridors are underserved.

BodaGo gives commuters a verified rider, a transparent fare estimate and a traceable trip. It gives riders a steady pipeline of trips and a tool to build a reputation.

## Target users

Primary users are urban and peri-urban commuters. Secondary users are motorbike riders and their SACCOs. Tertiary users are corporate clients who want to book rides for staff or delivery.

## MVP features

1. Rider sign up with ID and bike registration verification.
2. Passenger request with pickup and dropoff map pin and fare estimate.
3. Matching. The nearest available rider sees the request and accepts.
4. Live trip screen with pickup and dropoff times.
5. In app chat and call for coordination.
6. Trip rating and review by both parties.
7. Rider wallet. Platform takes a commission. Weekly payout.
8. Trip history for both sides.

## Post MVP ideas

Pre booked trips, delivery mode that routes to JiraniDelivery, corporate accounts, SACCO shared ownership of rider tools, in app insurance micro cover, safety camera with consent, SafeTrip Guardian link.

## User stories

As a commuter I want to see the price before I take a ride. As a rider I want fewer dead kilometers between jobs. As a SACCO manager I want to see my fleet activity and earnings.

## Success metrics

Verified riders, requests per day, acceptance rate, completed trips, average waiting time, cancellation rate.

## Revenue model

Commission per trip, rider subscription for premium placement, corporate billing.

## Risks and compliance

Verify rider ID and bike registration. Keep insurance disclosure clear. Do not record audio or video without consent. Allow a trip to be ended if either party feels unsafe. Comply with local transport regulation in each country.
