# TutorHub LocalTutors

A local tutor marketplace for parents and learners looking for one on one lessons.

## Problem

Parents who want a tutor for a struggling child often rely on word of mouth and end up with an unverified person who may or may not show up. Tutors who want paying clients cannot easily reach parents outside their immediate network. Price, availability and quality are all opaque.

TutorHub lets parents find, vet and book tutors for subjects, grades and languages they care about, with a clear service fee.

## Target users

Primary users are parents of primary and secondary learners. Secondary users are university students who tutor on the side. Tertiary users are tutoring centers that want an online storefront.

## MVP features

1. Tutor profile. Photo, subjects, grades, languages, experience, hourly rate, availability, references.
2. Verification. ID photo check, two references, admin review.
3. Search. By subject, location, language, availability.
4. Lesson request. Parent sends a request with preferred time, child grade and topic.
5. Messaging. In app chat.
6. Review and rating. After a confirmed lesson.
7. Booking fee. Platform takes a small service fee per paid lesson.

## Post MVP ideas

Trial lesson discount, group tutoring, live online classroom, learner progress tracking tied to ExamPrep AI, tutor payouts, background check integration.

## User stories

As a parent I want to find a verified Mathematics tutor who speaks Swahili within 5 kilometers of my home. As a tutor I want new paying clients without working through social media. As a learner I want a tutor who is patient with my pace.

## Success metrics

Verified tutors, lesson requests, paid bookings, repeat bookings, tutor retention.

## Revenue model

Service fee per paid booking. Tutor premium profile for featured placement. Tutoring center subscription.

## Risks and compliance

Verify tutors with ID and references. Protect minors. Require parent consent for learner profiles. Report abuse. Payments flow through a licensed provider in sandbox or live mode.
