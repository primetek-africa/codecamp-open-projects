# FloodReady Neighbour Alerts

## The idea
Help residents and local response groups share timely reports of flooding, blocked routes, and verified safe-route information. Begin with a single community or district.

## Users and value
Residents get local updates; response organizations can review reports and coordinate messaging. Revenue could come from an organization subscription or sponsored preparedness information.

## MVP scope
- Community reports with area, time, category, and optional evidence.
- Moderator verification and status updates.
- Area-based alert feed and blocked-route notes.
- Responder dashboard and report trend summary.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. A low-bandwidth web app with an area list can work before maps. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or Python, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not present unverified reports as official warnings or guarantee safe routes. Agree on moderation, escalation, and trusted local information sources before launch.
