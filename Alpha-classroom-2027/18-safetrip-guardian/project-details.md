# SafeTrip Guardian

A personal safety companion for trips taken on foot, by boda, matatu, danfo, long distance bus or private taxi.

## Problem

Women, students and night shift workers across African cities face real risks during routine trips. Sharing a live location with a friend requires WhatsApp or a third party app. There is no simple way to raise an alarm when connectivity is bad. Family members worry because they have no visibility into whether a loved one arrived.

SafeTrip Guardian lets a user share a trip with trusted contacts, check in at intervals and raise a panic alert with location, even on weak networks.

## Target users

Primary users are women commuters, students, delivery riders, night shift workers. Secondary users are parents and spouses.

## MVP features

1. Trip start. User enters destination and expected arrival time.
2. Trusted contacts. Up to 5 people receive a share link that updates with location.
3. Check in prompts. Periodic prompts during the trip. If missed, escalate.
4. Panic button. One tap sends an SMS and WhatsApp alert with last known location.
5. Quiet mode. Record audio when a panic is triggered and store locally with the option to upload after.
6. Trip history. Private log of past trips.

## Post MVP ideas

Family group plan, employer plan for delivery riders, community verified safe zone layer, integration with BodaGo and local emergency numbers.

## User stories

As a student taking a night matatu I want my mother to know I made it home. As a delivery rider I want my dispatcher to see I am stuck. As a parent I want a one button alert from my daughter.

## Success metrics

Active users, trips started per week, successful arrival confirmations, panic buttons used, false alarms.

## Revenue model

Freemium. Free tier allows one trip at a time. Paid family plan for multiple users and extended history. Employer plan for teams of 20 or more.

## Risks and compliance

Do not record audio without clear consent and a one time opt in at panic time. Protect location data at rest and in transit. Allow users to delete all data. Never send location to anyone outside the user's chosen contacts. Make clear the app is not a substitute for emergency services.

## Research references

UN Women urban safety reports, bSafe, Life360 and Noonlight as references, local women's safety audits in Nairobi, Lagos and Johannesburg.
