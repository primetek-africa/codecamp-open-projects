# ChamaLedger Savings Group

## The idea
Help a savings group keep shared records of member contributions, loans, repayments, and meeting decisions. The app records group activity but does not custody money.

## Users and value
Members can review agreed balances and group decisions; treasurers spend less time reconciling notebooks. Revenue may come from a group subscription.

## MVP scope
- Group setup, member register, and roles.
- Contribution, loan, and repayment ledger.
- Meeting minutes and recorded approvals.
- Member balance view and exportable statement.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Build a responsive web app usable on shared phones. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Agree how a group confirms entries and handles corrections. Do not collect member funds or claim to provide regulated lending, savings, or investment services.
