# SolarPay PAYG

A pay as you go subscription and service management platform for solar installers and their customers.

## Problem

Across Africa, millions of households and small businesses live off grid or with intermittent grid power. Solar systems from companies like M-KOPA, Sun King and d.light are sold on a PAYG basis. Smaller solar installers want to sell the same way but cannot build a custom platform. Customers cannot easily pay by mobile money or see their balance.

SolarPay gives a small installer a plug and play platform to register systems, collect PAYG payments and track service requests.

## Target users

Primary users are small to mid size solar installer companies. Secondary users are customers paying for a system over time. Tertiary users are financiers backing an installer.

## MVP features

1. Installer onboarding. Company profile, payout account.
2. System registration. Serial number, customer phone, tariff, duration.
3. PAYG schedule. Weekly or monthly installment.
4. Mobile money payment capture. Customer pays into the installer till. Payment is matched to the system via a reference.
5. Customer balance link. Short SMS with a link to a balance page.
6. Service request. Customer files a request, installer assigns a technician.
7. Reports. Active systems, collection rate, overdue.

## Post MVP ideas

Direct remote lock via IoT connected inverter, financing partner integration, customer app, inventory tracking, training content for technicians.

## User stories

As an installer I want to know which customers are behind. As a customer I want to see how much I still owe. As a technician I want a service request with the customer address.

## Success metrics

Active installers, systems under management, PAYG collection rate, service requests closed, retention after 6 months.

## Revenue model

SaaS fee per installer, percentage of collected PAYG, one time onboarding fee.

## Risks and compliance

Mobile money reconciliation must be accurate. Do not advertise remote shut off in the MVP unless the hardware supports it. Comply with consumer protection law when advertising repossession.
