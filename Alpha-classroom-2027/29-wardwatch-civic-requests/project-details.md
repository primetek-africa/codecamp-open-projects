# WardWatch Civic Requests

## The idea
Give residents a way to report local service issues and follow public updates from participating ward offices or service organizations. Begin with a few issue categories and one ward.

## Users and value
Residents can see whether an issue was received and what action is reported; local agencies can organize requests and publish responses. Revenue could come from a civic organization or agency subscription, not from selling resident data.

## MVP scope
- Issue reports with category, ward, description, and optional photo.
- Public or private reporter contact choice.
- Status timeline and agency response.
- Moderated, aggregated dashboard by issue category.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. A responsive web app with ward-level location is enough for the pilot. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Stay nonpartisan and make report status and agency participation clear. Moderate personal information, threats, and unverified allegations; never sell reporter data.
