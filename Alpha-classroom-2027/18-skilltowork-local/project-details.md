# SkillToWork Local

## The idea
Help entry-level workers show practical skills and find nearby paid work, including short projects and apprenticeships. Employers can shortlist candidates by skill and location.

## Users and value
Workers get a simple profile of experience and references; local employers spend less time finding candidates. Revenue could be employer subscriptions or a fee for featured listings.

## MVP scope
- Worker profile with skills, work samples, and optional references.
- Local job and short-task listings.
- Application and employer shortlist status.
- Report and moderation flow for suspicious listings.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Keep the application mobile-friendly and low-data. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not charge workers to apply or promise employment. Set a process for reviewing employers and reporting scams or discriminatory listings.
