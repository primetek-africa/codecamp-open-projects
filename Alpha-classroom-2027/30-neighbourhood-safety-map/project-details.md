# Neighbourhood Safety Map

## The idea
Let residents and local organizations share moderated, area-level summaries of hazards and safety resources. It is not a tool for identifying, tracking, or accusing individuals.

## Users and value
Residents can find community resources and understand reported area trends; organizations can identify common service needs. Revenue may come from an organization subscription or sponsored resource listings.

## MVP scope
- Anonymous hazard reports with broad area and category.
- Moderation queue and expiry date for time-sensitive reports.
- Area-level map or list and safety resource directory.
- Aggregate trends without identifying reporters or named individuals.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. A mobile-first web app can use an area list before adding a map. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or Python, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not publish names, faces, precise home locations, or accusations. Moderate reports, minimize retention, and make clear that the service is not an emergency-response channel.
