# BorderSend Remittance Tracker

## The idea
Help families and small traders compare publicly quoted costs and track a transfer arranged through a licensed remittance provider. The MVP is an information and record-keeping tool, not a money transfer service.

## Users and value
Senders can compare provider fees and track their own transfer references; providers can reach potential customers. Revenue could come from disclosed provider listings or referral partnerships.

## MVP scope
- Directory of participating, appropriately licensed providers.
- Provider-entered fee, exchange-rate quote, and quote timestamp.
- Private transfer tracker with reference and status updates.
- Receipt storage and support contact.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **6 to 8 weeks**. Build a responsive web app and use mock or manually entered data first. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Never receive, custody, or transmit funds. Validate provider licensing and local financial rules, show that quotes change, and avoid presenting user-entered transfer status as provider-confirmed.
