# TraderLoad Dispatch Board

## The idea
Match small traders with independent drivers who have capacity on local or regional trips. Focus on a defined corridor and a few types of goods.

## Users and value
Traders find transport options; drivers find loads that fit a planned trip. Revenue can come from a disclosed matching fee or business subscription.

## MVP scope
- Trader load requests with size, pickup, destination, and date.
- Driver and vehicle profiles with capacity and route.
- Match enquiry, trip offer, and acceptance.
- Delivery confirmation and commission record.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Start with area and route filters, not live tracking. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Verify participants and clarify that the platform is not an insurer or carrier. Agree how disputes, cancellations, and proof of delivery are handled.
