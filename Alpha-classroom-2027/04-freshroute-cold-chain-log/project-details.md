# FreshRoute Cold-Chain Log

## The idea
Make shipment handoffs and cooling checks visible to small produce suppliers, transporters, and buyers. The first version records checks entered by people rather than requiring sensors.

## Users and value
Suppliers can identify where handling problems occur; buyers receive a clearer delivery record. Charge per shipment or sell a small business subscription.

## MVP scope
- Create a shipment with product, quantity, destination, and expected arrival.
- Record handoffs between sender, transporter, and receiver.
- Enter temperature checks and flag missed or out-of-range checks.
- Confirm delivery and show a basic exception summary.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Use a phone-friendly web app that can queue check entries briefly when offline. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js, and MySQL or PostgreSQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not imply that manually entered records prove food safety. Sensor integrations, complex routing, and automated regulatory compliance are later work.
