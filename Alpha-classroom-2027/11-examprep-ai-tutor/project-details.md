# ExamPrep AI Tutor

An AI powered exam preparation platform for African national exams such as KCSE, WAEC, JAMB, BAC, NSC and BECE.

## Problem

Secondary school learners preparing for national exams face three barriers. One on one tutoring is too expensive for most families. Textbooks offer limited practice and rarely explain why an answer is wrong. Past paper banks exist on sites covered in ads and malware. At the same time, parents want to see their child making progress.

ExamPrep AI gives learners a personalized practice loop with AI generated explanations and curated past paper questions. It gives parents a visible progress dashboard.

## Target users

Primary users are secondary school learners aged 14 to 19. Secondary users are parents and guardians. Tertiary users are tutoring centers and schools that want to add digital practice to their program.

## MVP features

1. Subject and topic selection. Start with Mathematics, English, Biology, Physics, Chemistry for KCSE, WAEC and JAMB.
2. Practice session. 10 to 20 questions from a curated bank with multiple choice and short answer.
3. AI explanation. For every question, an explanation generated by an LLM and reviewed by a human teacher, with a fallback for questions already answered before.
4. Timed mock exam. One hour practice exam per subject with a score.
5. Score tracking. Per topic strength and weakness.
6. Parent progress view. A weekly summary link emailed or sent to a parent.
7. Offline friendly PWA with a cached last 100 questions.

## Post MVP ideas

Voice question read out in local languages, AI powered doubt chat limited to curriculum, live tutor booking, group study rooms, scholarship matching, school licensing.

## User stories

As a student I want to see why I got a question wrong so that I can avoid the same mistake. As a parent I want to know my child studied 3 hours this week in mathematics. As a tutor center director I want to deploy practice for 100 students at once.

## Success metrics

Active learners per week, questions attempted per session, mock exam completion, parent summary opens, retention through a 12 week program.

## Revenue model

Freemium. Free tier gives one subject with limited questions a day. Paid tier gives all subjects, mock exams and parent summary. School bulk license.

## Risks and compliance

AI content can be wrong. Mark explanations as AI generated with a reviewed stamp when a teacher has approved them. Do not use the AI as the only source for mock exam scoring. Keep learner data minimal. If learners are under 13, require a parent account.
