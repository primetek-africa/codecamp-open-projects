# SolarCare Service Desk

## The idea
Help households and small businesses using solar systems find an installer or technician for maintenance and repairs. The MVP manages service requests and follow-up, not remote equipment control.

## Users and value
Customers can explain a problem and see its status; technicians organize jobs and service history. Revenue could come from a monthly technician listing or a booking fee.

## MVP scope
- Technician profiles, service area, and supported system types.
- Customer service request with issue description and preferred time.
- Technician acceptance, appointment, and job status.
- Basic parts and service history for completed jobs.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. Build a mobile-first web app. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL. Use manual contact details before adding a payment integration.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not remotely control solar equipment or promise repair quality. Agree on technician screening and customer complaint handling for the pilot.
