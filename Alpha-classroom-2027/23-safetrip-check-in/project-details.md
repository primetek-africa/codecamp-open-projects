# SafeTrip Check-In

## The idea
Offer passengers a lightweight way to share a trip plan with trusted contacts and confirm arrival. The MVP uses voluntary check-ins, not live tracking.

## Users and value
Passengers have a simple check-in routine; transport operators may offer it as a customer feature. Revenue could come from an operator subscription or an optional individual plan.

## MVP scope
- Start a trip with destination area and expected arrival time.
- Choose trusted contacts and share a trip link or notification.
- Scheduled check-in and missed check-in escalation to chosen contacts.
- User-controlled trip history and deletion.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. Build a phone-first progressive web app. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js, and MySQL. Mock notifications first.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not promise emergency response or continuous location monitoring. Explain that contacts are responsible for responding and provide a clear stop and delete option.
