# NearbyMeds Stock Finder

## The idea
Let participating pharmacies publish whether selected common products are currently available, so customers can check before traveling. Pharmacies control their own updates.

## Users and value
Customers save time; pharmacies can attract local enquiries and manage product visibility. Revenue may come from a monthly pharmacy listing subscription.

## MVP scope
- Pharmacy profiles and service areas.
- Pharmacy-managed product catalogue and stock state.
- Search by product and area with a last-updated timestamp.
- Reservation enquiry, pharmacy response, and subscription status.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. A responsive web app is enough; keep catalogue search fast on mobile. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not recommend medicine, provide dosage advice, or imply stock is guaranteed. Limit listings to pharmacy-approved products and make availability time explicit.
