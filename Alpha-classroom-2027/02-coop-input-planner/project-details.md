# Co-op Input Planner

## The idea
Give a farmer cooperative one place to collect seasonal input needs and send a consolidated order to a supplier. This reduces repeated trips and helps the group see the order and payment status.

## Users and value
Co-op members and managers coordinate demand; suppliers receive more predictable orders. Revenue may be a cooperative subscription or a disclosed supplier referral fee.

## MVP scope
- Co-op membership and manager-managed member records.
- Seasonal catalogue with supplier, item, unit, price, and availability.
- Member quantities submitted by a deadline and consolidated group order.
- Payment status recorded by a manager and exportable supplier summary.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **4 to 6 weeks**. A responsive web app works on phones and shared computers. Optional stack: Vue or React, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL. Keep supplier payments outside the app for the pilot.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not recommend regulated pesticides or promise supplier availability. Confirm product details, local seasons, and group approval steps with a cooperative.
