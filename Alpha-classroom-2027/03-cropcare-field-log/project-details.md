# CropCare Field Log

## The idea
Help growers keep a record of crop symptoms and connect with locally qualified agricultural advisers. The product organizes observations; it does not diagnose crops or replace extension services.

## Users and value
Farmers maintain plot histories. Advisers can review better-structured reports and offer paid consultations or cooperative plans.

## MVP scope
- Farmer, crop, and plot records.
- Symptom reports with date, optional photo, and observed conditions.
- Adviser-reviewed guidance cards with source and last-reviewed date.
- Referral request and follow-up reminder.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Build a mobile-friendly web app with compressed image uploads. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or Python, and MySQL or MongoDB.

## Keep out of the first release
Do not generate treatment instructions automatically or recommend chemical dosages. Have qualified local advisers review all guidance and make referral limitations clear.
