# RentalVerify Platform

A landlord and tenant management platform for urban rentals.

## Problem

Urban housing in Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Kampala and Johannesburg is plagued by fake listings, fake agents and rent paid in cash with no paper trail. Tenants lose deposits. Landlords get stuck with bad tenants and cannot collect. Maintenance requests disappear in WhatsApp threads.

RentalVerify gives landlords verified listings, tenant background checks and digital rent collection. It gives tenants trust in a listing and a way to request maintenance.

## Target users

Primary users are landlords of one to 20 units. Secondary users are caretakers and letting agents. Tertiary users are prospective and current tenants.

## MVP features

1. Landlord onboarding. ID, property deed photo, admin review.
2. Property listing with photos and location.
3. Tenant application. ID, work reference, previous landlord reference.
4. Rent schedule. Monthly amount, due date.
5. Mobile money rent capture with reference.
6. Receipt generation.
7. Maintenance request with photo and status.
8. Dispute flag with admin mediator.

## Post MVP ideas

Direct rent collection via Paystack subscriptions, deposit escrow with a licensed partner, tenant credit history that improves next application, apartment viewing booking, utility bill tracking.

## User stories

As a landlord I want proof that my tenant paid rent so I never argue again. As a tenant I want a trail that shows I pay on time so my next landlord trusts me. As a caretaker I want maintenance requests in one place.

## Success metrics

Verified landlords, active units, rent collected on time, maintenance requests closed, deposit disputes resolved.

## Revenue model

Platform fee per rent collected, premium listing, verification fee.

## Risks and compliance

Verify both landlord and tenant to prevent fraud. Use a licensed payment provider. Keep ID images encrypted. Comply with each country's data protection act.

## Research references

AirBnB verification flows, SweepSouth and OPay studies on gig work trust, HassConsult reports, Lamudi and Jiji data on listings fraud.
