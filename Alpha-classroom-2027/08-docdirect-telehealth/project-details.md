# DocDirect Telehealth

A booking and video consultation platform that connects patients with licensed clinicians.

## Problem

Access to a qualified doctor remains uneven across Africa. A patient in a rural town or a small city may need to travel hours for a short routine consultation. Clinicians who could serve remote patients lack a simple booking and consultation tool that works on cheap phones and low bandwidth connections.

DocDirect gives patients a way to book, consult over video and pay a licensed clinician. It gives clinicians a lightweight schedule, consultation workspace and payout record.

## Target users

Primary users are patients outside major cities with a smartphone. Secondary users are licensed general practitioners, pediatricians, dermatologists, mental health professionals and midwives who want remote clients.

## MVP features

1. Clinician verification. Upload of medical council registration number, ID photo, admin review.
2. Availability. Clinician sets weekly slots.
3. Patient booking. Choose specialty, see available clinicians, pick slot.
4. In app video consultation. Low bandwidth friendly with audio only fallback.
5. Consultation notes. Clinician writes a short note that the patient can view.
6. Payment. Patient pays via mobile money in advance. Clinician is paid out weekly minus platform fee.
7. Follow up reminder. Reminder for a next step like a lab test or in person visit.

## Post MVP ideas

Lab test booking, prescription with a verified handoff to PharmaFind, consultation record export, insurance claim submission, group consultations for family planning or chronic clinics.

## User stories

As a patient in a small town I want a short video consultation with a doctor without traveling. As a clinician I want to see my paid bookings at a glance. As an admin I want to verify a clinician in under a day.

## Success metrics

Verified clinicians, booked and completed consultations, average consultation duration, patient satisfaction, no show rate.

## Revenue model

Platform fee per paid consultation. Clinician subscription for a featured profile and more slots. Employer or insurer bulk plan.

## Risks and compliance

Clinicians must be verified against the relevant medical council register. Do not allow prescription of controlled substances through the MVP. Encrypt notes at rest. Comply with the data protection act in each country. Make clear that telehealth is not a replacement for emergency care.
