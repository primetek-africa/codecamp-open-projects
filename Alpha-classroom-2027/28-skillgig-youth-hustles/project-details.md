# SkillGig Youth Hustles

A short gig marketplace that connects entry level youth with small businesses, NGOs and households.

## Problem

Youth unemployment is a crisis across the continent. Many young people have practical skills from informal apprenticeship but no way to show them. Small employers who need a plumber, a graphic designer, a mover, a social media freelancer or a tutor rely on word of mouth and often cannot find anyone.

SkillGig gives youth a profile with portfolio and verified references and gives small employers a safe way to hire for short gigs.

## Target users

Primary users are university students, out of school youth aged 18 to 28 and informal apprentices. Secondary users are SMEs, NGOs and households.

## MVP features

1. Worker profile. Photo, skills, portfolio photos, references.
2. Reference verification. Admin or automated call to a listed referee.
3. Gig listings by employer. Category, location, budget, time window.
4. Application flow. Short message and proposed rate.
5. Employer shortlist and messaging.
6. Milestone marker. Employer marks gig as started, completed.
7. Payment capture. Employer pays via mobile money, platform takes a fee.
8. Review on both sides.

## Post MVP ideas

Escrow with a licensed partner, insurance microcover for risky gigs, training modules tied to each skill, employer scoring to filter abusive posters, community verified groups.

## User stories

As a youth I want to find my first paid gig. As a household I want to hire a trusted mover for a Saturday. As an NGO I want to hire 10 field assistants for a week.

## Success metrics

Verified workers, gig listings, paid completions, repeat employers, average gig value.

## Revenue model

Service fee per paid gig, worker premium profile, employer subscription for bulk hiring.

## Risks and compliance

Keep minors off the platform. Verify workers. Clear safety guidance for in person gigs. Payments through a licensed provider in sandbox or live mode.
