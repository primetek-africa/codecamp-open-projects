# DiasporaSend Remittance Hub

A comparison and tracking platform for cross border remittances to African countries.

## Problem

Africans in the diaspora send more than 100 billion US dollars home every year. They use providers like WorldRemit, Sendwave, Remitly, LemFi, Western Union, MoneyGram, Chipper, Afriex and Nala. The true cost of a transfer is hidden behind the mid market rate, hidden spreads, fixed fees and promotions. Once a transfer is sent, there is often no single place to check its status without logging into each provider.

DiasporaSend helps a sender compare providers fairly for the exact corridor and amount and track a transfer end to end.

## Target users

Primary users are African diaspora senders in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Germany, France and the Gulf. Secondary users are small cross border traders. Tertiary users are financial journalists and NGOs who want corridor data.

## MVP features

1. Corridor rate comparison. User picks send country, receive country, amount. The app returns provider rate, fee, time to arrival and total amount received.
2. Provider directory. One profile per provider with payment methods, delivery methods, licensing jurisdiction and user rating.
3. Send simulation. A step by step guided checklist that opens the provider site in a new tab. No money moves through DiasporaSend.
4. Transfer tracker. Sender enters the provider name, reference and amount. The app shows a status timeline either automatically where provider APIs allow, or manually updated by the sender.
5. Alerts. SMS or email alert when a provider rate is better than a user set threshold.
6. Review and rating per corridor.

## Post MVP ideas

Direct API integration where providers allow, white label embed for diaspora associations, partnership payouts via mobile money APIs, corridor insights dashboard sold to media houses.

## User stories

As a sender in London I want to know which provider will give my sister in Nairobi the most shillings today so that I stop losing money to hidden spreads. As a journalist I want to see six month fee trends for GBP to GHS so I can write a story.

## Success metrics

Monthly active comparison searches, provider referral clicks, number of tracked transfers, alert conversion rate.

## Revenue model

Affiliate commissions from partner providers on referred transfers. Premium alerts subscription. Data licensing for corridor insights.

## Risks and compliance

Do not route funds through DiasporaSend. Be clear that the app is informational. Provider rates must come from a verifiable source such as the provider site or a signed partner feed. Include a disclaimer that rates change by the minute.
