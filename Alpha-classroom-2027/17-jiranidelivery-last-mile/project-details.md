# JiraniDelivery Last-Mile

A last mile delivery dispatch platform for neighborhood shops, restaurants and small businesses.

## Problem

A neighborhood shop or home baker can take an online order, but cannot afford to build a dispatch team. Delivery platforms charge high commission or do not serve small merchants. The result is lost sales or self delivery, which does not scale.

JiraniDelivery gives a small merchant a pool of nearby couriers, auto dispatch and simple tracking, at a fair commission.

## Target users

Primary users are small retail shops, restaurants, bakers, florists, salons and pharmacies. Secondary users are independent couriers on motorbikes or bicycles. Tertiary users are small e-commerce sellers.

## MVP features

1. Merchant onboarding. Business profile, pickup address, bank or mobile money for payouts.
2. Courier onboarding. ID, vehicle type, insurance disclosure, availability toggle.
3. Order entry. Merchant adds an order with customer phone, dropoff address and items.
4. Dispatch. Platform auto assigns the nearest available courier. Courier can accept or decline.
5. Tracking. Customer sees status and courier ETA via a share link.
6. Proof of delivery. Photo or signature on the courier phone.
7. Settlement. Weekly or daily payout, platform fee held back.

## Post MVP ideas

Direct integration with LocalEats, courier insurance microcover, batch deliveries, scheduled pickups, cold chain delivery for pharmacies.

## User stories

As a baker I want to accept orders without hiring a rider. As a courier I want enough jobs between my regular runs to make a day worth it. As a customer I want to know where my order is without calling.

## Success metrics

Active merchants, couriers online per day, completed deliveries, average delivery time, dispute rate, payout speed.

## Revenue model

Commission per delivery, courier subscription for premium placement, merchant subscription for priority dispatch.

## Risks and compliance

Verify couriers. Keep customer phone numbers hidden from couriers after delivery. Comply with transport and ecommerce rules. Do not store card data.
