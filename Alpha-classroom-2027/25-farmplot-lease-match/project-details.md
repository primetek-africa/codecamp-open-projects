# FarmPlot Lease Match

## The idea
Help landowners with unused agricultural plots connect with farmers seeking seasonal land. Start with one locality and make location, season, size, and terms clear before an enquiry.

## Users and value
Landowners find potential renters; farmers discover available plots. Revenue can come from a listing fee or a subscription for land agents and cooperatives.

## MVP scope
- Owner and farmer profiles.
- Plot listings with approximate area, season, water access, and asking terms.
- Search by area and season with an enquiry flow.
- Lease checklist and status tracking.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Build a responsive app and use approximate map areas rather than public exact boundaries. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
The platform is not a land registry or legal adviser. Encourage written agreements and locally appropriate checks; do not claim ownership has been verified unless it has.
