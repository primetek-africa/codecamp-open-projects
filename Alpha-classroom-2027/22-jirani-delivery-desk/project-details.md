# Jirani Delivery Desk

## The idea
Give neighborhood shops a simple way to assign local deliveries to available couriers and keep customers updated. Start with a few shops and a small service area.

## Users and value
Shops can offer delivery without building their own dispatch system; couriers receive more work. Revenue may be a per-delivery coordination fee or shop subscription.

## MVP scope
- Merchant order entry with delivery area and customer contact.
- Courier availability and manual delivery assignment.
- Delivery status updates and customer reference.
- Daily delivery and fee summary.

## Delivery and optional technology
Estimated MVP: **5 to 7 weeks**. Use a responsive app and manual assignment before testing automation. Optional stack: React or Vue, Node.js or PHP, and MySQL.

## Keep out of the first release
Avoid storing unnecessary customer data and do not track couriers continuously. Define delivery limits, cancellation rules, and responsibility with pilot merchants.
